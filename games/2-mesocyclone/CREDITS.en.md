## Photo textures — Poly Haven (CC0)

The photo textures in `Assets/_Game/Textures/External/` come from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and are released under the [CC0 1.0 public-domain dedication](https://polyhaven.com/license). Attribution is not required; it is provided here with thanks. The source maps include diffuse/albedo, OpenGL normal and AO/roughness/metal textures. Derived maps are generated from these sources by the project's editor code.
